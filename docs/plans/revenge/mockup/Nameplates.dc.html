Revenge only adds the skull, placed beside whichever nameplate is showing: the game's own or an addon's (Threat Plates, Plater, Platynator, Kui and others). It never resizes, recolours or replaces the plate.
The skull goes away when the kills expire or are dismissed. It can be switched off.
