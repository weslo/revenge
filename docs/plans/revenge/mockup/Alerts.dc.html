Popup: small, near the top of the screen. Silent, no screen-wide effects. Fades after about 4 seconds, or at once when clicked (the second one is fading).
Killer nearby: when a recent killer shows up on a nameplate, your target or under the mouse. At most once every 5 minutes per player. The sound is for chat only and can be switched off.
Friend or guildmate killed: as soon as their death is shared, wherever it happened in the open world. Once per death.
